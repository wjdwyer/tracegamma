# cran-comments

## Submission

`tracegamma` 0.1.1 — first submission to CRAN.

A reference distribution for quadratic-form test statistics: the closed-form
shifted, scaled chi-square matched to the first three cumulants of a quadratic
form in near-normal variables. Welch's F and Pearson's chi-square are exact
special cases rather than competitors. The package also provides the exact
generalized chi-square by Imhof (1961) inversion, so the closed form can be
checked against the truth rather than against simulation.
